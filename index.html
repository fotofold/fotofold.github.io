FotoFold (iteration 18)

Fold it, photograph it, hear it.

You need four strips of paper, a pen, and the edge of a card. Each set starts by folding a whole into equal parts.

Who is using this phone?
If your teacher gave you a class code and a number, type them here. No names.

Settings
inches, measured with a ruler

FotoFold only uses the camera. It never opens your photos, and it never keeps the photo it takes. Your answers stay on this phone, so a sleeping screen doesn't lose them, until you tap Start fresh on the first screen. In a live class, your number and your answers also go to your teacher's screen. Never your name, never a photo.
